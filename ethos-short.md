My ethos as a Technical Director is grounded in technical stewardship, mission impact, and service to the user. I see the role as part architect, part advisor, part mentor, and part connector—responsible for setting clear technical direction, developing people, and ensuring technology reduces burden, empowers users, and advances the mission. I value pragmatism over architectural purity, simplicity over unnecessary complexity, and deliberate evolution over preserving systems simply because they already exist. Security and usability are non-negotiable, experimentation should be encouraged, and proven approaches should be standardized. Staff must own the architecture, vendors should provide capabilities rather than define it, and technical debt should only be accepted intentionally and with a plan to repay it. I believe technical authority should be exercised when necessary, but saying no always carries an obligation to find another path. Above all, I aim to lead with expertise, accountability, continuous learning, and a commitment to leaving behind stronger people, better systems, and an architecture worth inheriting.
